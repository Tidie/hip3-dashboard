Overview Tab

The main dashboard providing a bird's eye view of the HIP-3 ecosystem.

The Overview tab is the first thing you see when you load the tracker. It displays aggregate statistics and charts covering all HIP-3 deployed markets across every deployer (DEX builder) on Hyperliquid.

  • Stat Cards — Total Markets, 24h Volume, Total Open Interest, Unique Deployers, and Estimated 24h Fees are shown at the top.
  • Daily Trading Volume — Stacked bar chart showing volume per deployer per day. Supports Daily and Cumulative modes, a zoom range selector, and mouse wheel zoom. Excludes liquidation volume.
  • Daily Trades Count — Same format as volume but tracks the number of individual trades per deployer per day.
  • Daily Fees — Estimated fees generated per deployer per day, calculated as volume × 0.01%.
  • Market Share — Doughnut chart with tabs for Volume, Trades, and Open Interest. Shows each deployer's share with logos and percentages.
  • Top Markets by Volume — Horizontal bar chart of the 12 most traded tickers in the last 24h.
  • OI vs Volume Bubble — Scatter plot where bubble size represents Open Interest, helping identify markets with unusual OI-to-volume ratios.
  • Deployer Performance — Grouped bar chart comparing each deployer's volume and Open Interest.
  • Fees by Deployer / by Type — Breakdown of estimated fee revenue by builder and by asset category (Crypto, Stocks, Commodities, Indices).

Markets Tab

Browse and search all listed HIP-3 markets.

The Markets tab provides a searchable, sortable table of every market deployed through HIP-3. Each row shows the ticker, deployer (with logo), category, 24h volume, open interest, mark price, funding rate, and 24h price change.

  • Search — Filter markets by ticker name in real time.
  • Category Filters — Filter by All, Crypto, Stocks, Commodities, or Indices.
  • Sortable Columns — Click any column header to sort ascending or descending.
  • Deployer Logos — Each market displays its deployer's logo for quick identification.

Compare Tab

Side-by-side comparison of up to 4 markets.

Select up to 4 markets from the dropdown selectors to compare them across all key metrics. The comparison view displays volume, open interest, funding rate, mark price, and other data points in a visual grid format, making it easy to evaluate markets against each other.

Funding Rate Heatmap Tab

Visual overview of historical funding rates across all HIP-3 markets.

The heatmap displays funding rates as annualized percentages (APR) for the top 30 markets by volume. Each cell represents one hour of funding data, color-coded from purple (very negative) through teal (neutral) to red (very positive).

  • Time Periods — Switch between 1 Day, 2 Days, 7 Days, and 30 Days of historical data.
  • Dual-Handle Contrast — Drag the two handles on the color bar to filter which funding rates are visible. Everything outside the selected range becomes dark, letting you isolate extreme positive or negative rates.
  • Stats Columns — Each row shows the Current funding rate, 24h Average, and 7-Day Average on the right side.
  • Hover Tooltip — Mouse over any cell to see the exact funding rate, date/time, and ticker.

Color Scale

Purple/Blue = negative (shorts pay longs) · Teal = neutral · Green/Yellow/Orange/Red = positive (longs pay shorts). Colors are absolute — +50% APR is always the same color regardless of the range filter.

Risk Score Tab

Health assessment of each HIP-3 market.

The Risk Score tab assigns a health grade to each market based on a weighted composite of several on-chain and off-chain metrics. The overview table shows all markets sorted by score, with colored grade badges.

  • Grades — A+ (Excellent) through F (Dangerous), calculated from a 0–100 score.
  • Factors — Oracle reliability, liquidity depth, 24h volume, open interest, funding rate stability, and spread analysis all contribute to the score.
  • Detail View — Click any market to see a full breakdown with individual factor scores, gauge visualization, and specific risk indicators.

Scoring Methodology

The score is a weighted average: Volume (25%), Open Interest (20%), Liquidity (20%), Oracle Quality (20%), Funding Stability (15%). Markets with no oracle or very low liquidity receive automatic penalties.

Liquidation Dashboard Tab

Real-time tracking of liquidations across HIP-3 markets.

The Liquidation tab connects to the Hyperliquid WebSocket feed and displays liquidation events as they happen. It provides both aggregate stats and a live event feed.

  • Live Stats — Total liquidations in the last 24h, largest single liquidation, total liquidated volume, and count of affected addresses.
  • Real-Time Feed — Each liquidation event appears with the ticker, side (long/short), size, price, and the liquidated address (truncated).
  • WebSocket Connection — Data streams in real time with automatic reconnection. The connection status is shown at the top.

Airdrop Tracker Tab

Leaderboard and personal tracking for HIP-3 trading activity.

The Tracker tab features a leaderboard of all addresses that have traded on HIP-3 markets, ranked by total volume. It also allows individual address lookup to see personal stats.

  • Leaderboard — Sortable by Volume, Fees, PnL, or Trades. Supports pagination with 10/20/50/100 rows per page across 90,000+ unique traders.
  • Address Search — Enter any Ethereum address to see its total volume, fees paid, PnL, number of trades, and pairs traded.
  • Fee Estimation — Fees are calculated as volume × 0.01% based on HIP-3's fee structure.

Data Sources & Methodology

How the data is collected, processed, and displayed.

Hyperliquid API

Real-time market data (prices, volume, OI, funding) is fetched from the Hyperliquid Info API. Data refreshes automatically every 60 seconds.

S3 Historical Data

Historical fill data is synced hourly from Hyperliquid's public S3 bucket (hl-mainnet-node-data). Each file contains one hour of fills across all markets. The sync bot filters for HIP-3 fills and stores them in Supabase.

Deduplication

Each fill has a unique (tid, address) composite key. Duplicate fills from overlapping S3 files are automatically ignored on insert, ensuring accurate volume and trade counts.

Liquidation Exclusion

All volume, trade count, and fee charts explicitly exclude liquidation events. Liquidations are tracked separately in the Liquidation Dashboard.

Deployer Identification

HIP-3 market coins are formatted as deployer:TICKER. The deployer prefix is used to attribute volume, trades, and fees to each DEX builder.
